# OpenVerge

OpenVerge is a community of Developers from various technical backgrounds who are passionate about creating open-source software solutions. We learn, share knowledge, and collaborate on projects that benefit the wider tech community.

## Our Mission
To foster a collaborative environment where developers can contribute to open-source projects, share their expertise, and grow their skills while making a positive impact on the tech ecosystem.

We are still in the early stages of building our community and projects. Stay tuned for updates as we develop our platform and launch exciting new initiatives!

As we are a bunch of novice developers, feel free to criticize our work and provide suggestions for improvement. Your feedback is invaluable to us as we strive to learn and grow together.
